# TutorBot English AI v1.0.0

The first full release: a free, open-source English course and PTE Academic practice app that runs on your own
computer. No cloud account, no API keys and no subscription.

> Practice estimates only. Nobody can guarantee a PTE score. TutorBot English AI is not affiliated with, endorsed by
> or sponsored by Pearson. PTE and PTE Academic are trademarks of Pearson.

## What you get
- **English course:** 6 levels (pre-A1 to C1+), 95 lessons in 21 units, end-of-unit tests, placement test,
  1,561-word vocabulary track and pronunciation drills.
- **PTE practice for all 22 question types:** a guide page per type, 446 original questions, instant marking for
  reading and listening, AI feedback for speaking and writing, timed drills, and a mistake notebook with spaced
  review.
- **Mock exams:** full, section and mini mocks with timers, resume after a reload, no going back, and a report by
  section, skill and type.
- **Personal coach:** next steps from your own answers, adaptive difficulty, readiness guide (bands, not scores),
  weekly check-in and a daily plan.
- **Tutor chat** at your level. It stays on English and PTE topics and never predicts a score.
- **Progress charts** with data tables and a CSV report.
- **Admin area:** content review and publishing (AI-drafted content needs a human approval), learner reports,
  uploads, user roles, mock exam set-up, usage monitoring and an AI calibration tool.
- **Privacy:** everything stays on your computer. Export or delete your data at any time; `make backup` and
  `make restore` for the whole install.

## Install
See the README: `make setup`, then `make start`, then open http://localhost:3000.

## Known limitations
- Tested on Linux only. Windows (WSL2) and macOS are supported by the scripts but not tested.
- AI runs in demo mode until you install the local models; the local models were not run in our test environment.
- Pronunciation feedback uses speech-recognition confidence, not a phoneme model.
- No 10-90 score estimates. Calibration must reach its minimum first, and switching them on is a separate decision.
- Some mock exam rules (item counts, section clocks) are not published by Pearson and are marked unverified.
- Full list: STATUS.md.
