# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, especially with learners and beginners. No harassment, insults or
discrimination. Many people here are learning English, so please write simply and never mock language mistakes.

Report problems privately to the maintainers (see SECURITY.md for the contact method). Maintainers may remove
content or block people who break these rules.
